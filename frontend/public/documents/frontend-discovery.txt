FRONTEND DISCOVERY CHECKLIST
Local sample resource for learning. Not a completed client assessment.

1. Who is the primary user?
2. What task should they be able to complete?
3. What information do they need at each step?
4. Which constraints are confirmed, and which are assumptions?
5. What does an empty, invalid, or unavailable state look like?
6. Can the journey be completed with a keyboard?
7. Does the layout remain readable at small widths and high zoom?
8. What is intentionally outside the current scope?
9. What would a useful manual walkthrough demonstrate?
10. Which decisions need to be recorded before extending the project?

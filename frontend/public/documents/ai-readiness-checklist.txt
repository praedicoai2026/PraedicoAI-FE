AI OPPORTUNITY READINESS CHECKLIST
Workshop aid. Not a compliance or security certification.

PROBLEM FIT
[ ] The task and current workflow are described in plain language.
[ ] A real user and accountable process owner are identified.
[ ] The problem matters enough to justify changing the workflow.
[ ] A simpler rule, search, interface, or process change was considered.

INFORMATION
[ ] Required information sources and their owners are known.
[ ] Source quality, freshness, and contradictions have been reviewed.
[ ] Data access and user permissions are understood.
[ ] Sensitive and prohibited information is identified.
[ ] Privacy, retention, and provider questions have an owner.

RISK AND HUMAN CONTROL
[ ] Consequences of a wrong or incomplete result are understood.
[ ] Decisions that must remain with a person are explicit.
[ ] Reviewers can inspect relevant evidence and correct a result.
[ ] Escalation, fallback, and stop conditions are defined.
[ ] Abuse, misuse, and out-of-scope requests are considered.

EVALUATION
[ ] Representative test cases can be prepared without exposing secrets.
[ ] Expected behavior and acceptance criteria are written down.
[ ] Failure cases include uncertainty, missing data, and conflicting sources.
[ ] Review effort and error cost will be considered alongside output quality.
[ ] A named decision owner will decide whether to continue or stop.

DELIVERY READINESS
[ ] Integration boundaries and dependencies have been identified.
[ ] Operational ownership, monitoring, and incident handling are discussed.
[ ] Model/provider selection will follow the use case and constraints.
[ ] Pilot scope is small, reversible, and proportionate to risk.
[ ] The next decision and evidence needed are agreed.

If important items are unknown, capture them as discovery work. Do not treat
this checklist as an approval to process sensitive or production information.

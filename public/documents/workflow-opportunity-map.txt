WORKFLOW OPPORTUNITY MAP
Workshop worksheet for understanding a process before automating it.

PROCESS
Name and purpose: [What outcome should the process produce?]
Start trigger: [What starts the work?]
End condition: [How does an owner know it is complete?]
Process owner: [Who is accountable for the whole process?]

MAP EACH STEP
Step: [What happens?]
Role: [Who performs it?]
Input: [What information or evidence is needed?]
Decision: [What judgment or rule is applied?]
System: [Which tool or record is used?]
Handoff: [Who receives the work next?]
Wait or rework: [What causes delay, repetition, or missing context?]
Exception: [What can go wrong and who handles it?]

OPPORTUNITY REVIEW
[ ] Is the step repetitive and well understood?
[ ] Could a rule or better interface solve it more predictably?
[ ] Would AI add useful flexibility for variable information?
[ ] Is the required information approved, current, and accessible?
[ ] What must a person check or approve?
[ ] What happens when information is missing or contradictory?
[ ] How will an exception be routed and tracked?
[ ] What evidence would show the change helped?

PROPOSED NEXT STEP
Smallest workflow slice: [One task and one user group]
Change to explore: [Process, software, AI assistance, or a combination]
Owner and reviewer: [Named responsibilities]
Risks and assumptions: [Separate facts from items to verify]
Measure and review trigger: [What evidence changes the next decision?]

Use non-confidential examples in this worksheet. It is not an implementation
plan or authorization to automate a controlled or safety-critical process.
